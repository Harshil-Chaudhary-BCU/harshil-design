Designing beyond the
screen

I’m Harshil, a product designer interested in how people interact with technology.

I work across digital products, automotive interfaces and immersive experiences. Across all three, I keep coming back to the same question: how can the experience feel clearer, more useful and more natural to the person using it?

[01]

Product Design

I turn complex problems into clear, usable product experiences. My work runs from early flows and wireframes through to high-fidelity interfaces and prototypes. I focus on structure, interaction and the decisions behind the experience, as well as how the final screen looks.

[02]

Research & Testing

I use research to understand what works in an experience, where people run into difficulties and what needs to change. My process includes interviews, surveys, usability testing, think-aloud sessions and structured evaluation. I use what I learn to make practical changes to the design.

[03]

Automotive & Spatial

I’m especially interested in experiences that go beyond traditional screens. My work explores automotive HMI, AR interfaces and immersive environments. I think about how information and interaction change when an interface becomes part of a vehicle or physical space.

[04]

Prototyping & Technology

My background in computer science helps me understand how the technology behind an experience works. I use prototypes to make ideas tangible, explore interactions and communicate concepts across digital, automotive and immersive products.

Key Principles

What I believe in

“I believe the best design comes from understanding the bigger picture and looking beyond the screen in front of you.”

Design the system, not just the screen

I like to understand how people, flows, technology, constraints and context fit together. The interface is one part of that wider experience.

Make it real early

I’d rather prototype, test and learn than hold on to an idea for too long. Building something helps me find questions that are easy to miss when we only talk about it.

Own the outcome

For me, design continues beyond handing over screens. I stay close to the problem, explain my decisions clearly and keep working on the idea until it becomes something useful.

Keep looking beyond the obvious

I’m drawn to unfamiliar problems and new ways of interacting, from automotive interfaces to immersive environments. My strongest ideas usually start with curiosity.
