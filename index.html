Designing beyond the
screen

I’m Harshil, a product designer interested in the space where people, technology and interaction meet.

My work moves across digital products, automotive interfaces and immersive experiences, but the thing I’m always trying to understand is the same: how can this experience feel clearer, more useful and more natural to the person using it?

[01]

Product Design

I turn complex problems into clear, usable product experiences — moving from early flows and wireframes to high-fidelity interfaces and prototypes. I focus on structure, interaction and the decisions behind the experience, not just how the final screen looks.

[02]

Research & Testing

I use research to understand where an experience works, where it creates friction and what needs to change. My process includes interviews, surveys, usability testing, think-aloud sessions and structured evaluation — turning findings into practical design iterations.

[03]

Automotive & Spatial

I’m especially interested in experiences that go beyond traditional screens. My work explores automotive HMI, AR interfaces and immersive environments — thinking about how information and interaction change when the interface becomes part of a vehicle or physical space.

[04]

Prototyping & Technology

My computer science background helps me understand the technology behind the experience. I use prototyping to make ideas tangible, explore interactions and communicate concepts across digital, automotive and immersive products.

Key Principles

I follow and believe in

“I believe the best design comes from understanding the bigger picture — not just the screen in front of you.”

Design the system, not just the screen

I like understanding how the whole experience connects — people, flows, technology, constraints and context. The interface is only one part of the product.

Make it real early

I’d rather prototype, test and learn than spend too long protecting an idea. Making something tangible exposes the questions that discussion alone usually cannot.

Own the outcome

I don’t see design as handing over screens. I stay close to the problem, communicate decisions clearly and push the idea until it becomes something useful.

Keep looking beyond the obvious

I’m drawn to unfamiliar problems and emerging interactions — from automotive interfaces to immersive environments. Curiosity is usually where my strongest ideas begin.
